# ALLY DOCKING — harbors

RULED by Adam 2026-10-22; design accepted, build queued
after DAMAGE_CONTROL.md's engine proves out. Its own slice —
not folded into Damage Control.

- An allied ship may dock with a friendly **Cruiser,
  Carrier, or Hauler** for repair benefit. Docked = attached:
  rides the host, out of combat — cannot fire, cannot be hit.
- **Benefit = a HOST MULTIPLIER on the guest's repair RATE**
  — DRAFT Cruiser ×1.25, Hauler ×1.5, Carrier ×2, with a mild
  climb by tier. **The guest's repair CAP stays its own
  hull's law:** a host speeds repair; it never raises the
  ceiling.
- **Capacity DRAFT:** Cruiser 1; Hauler 1 (2 from T4);
  Carrier 1 / 2 / 2 / 3 by tier ("maybe 3 allies max at
  Tier 5 Carrier/Mothership" — Adam).
- **The host's hold pays** → consent is a mechanic: hosts
  carry a **HARBOR switch** (human hosts may close, refuse,
  or undock a guest; bot hosts default ON by B2 policy —
  BOTS.md). Harbor closing mid-repair = gentle undock at
  current health; host death = spill at the wreck (escort
  law, DAMAGE_CONTROL.md).
- **The guest's repair toggle stays the guest's:** docked
  with repair OFF = a PASSENGER — shelter first, workshop
  second.
- **Eligibility reads off TEAM MEMBERSHIP, not mode** (Adam's
  correction): *"Even in Solo, unless it's 1v1, there could
  be allies who wish to dock."* A solo seat with bot
  teammates gets the full harbor surface, and a bot ally may
  sit in the guest manifest; bot guests seek allied harbors
  under BOTS.md posture logic (falling back to dock and
  repair). Only pure 1v1 / true FFA shows no harbor.
- Surfaces: HARBOR block in the frame panel (switch,
  capacity, guest manifest with per-guest UNDOCK), a
  contextual DOCK pill near a friendly open harbor, a guest
  DOCKED chip (REPAIRING vs PASSENGER) + UNDOCK. Key
  placement happens in this slice — E's old dock reservation
  was released when E took ITEM (CONTROLS.md); the
  contextual pill is the primary surface in any case.
