# SIGNATURE ABILITIES — one per line, no other line gets it

DESIGN STAGE. Adam commissioned the concept 2026-10-22, with
his words attached: "these aren't law." Nothing builds before
he strikes BackGround's full read.

The frame: one ability per line that NO other line gets;
Skiff gets none. Born minuscule at T2; escalates T2→T5 on
three axes — **potency, charges (count), charge regen**.
Charges BANK: saved for the moment, not cooldown babysitting.
The ability's key is C (CONTROLS.md).

## FIGHTER — LIGHTNING LOCK

Adam's replacement for a discarded salvo concept (per-weapon
keys with separate reloads — primary ~170 ms — already ≈
salvo, so a salvo signature duplicated the base kit). A
lightning weapon that AUTO-TARGETS: target selection
switchable (Tab cycles, click/tap sets — CONTROLS.md), the
ship turns to fire at the lock automatically for a few
seconds (duration grows with tier); the bolt "increasingly
more vicious, bigger, wider, fiercer" (chain forks proposed
at T4+); a chance to KNOCK OUT ship or turret systems for a
duration. Proposed fences: the lock respects stealth
geometry — cannot lock what it cannot see, and VANISH (below)
is its hard counter; CME hardening resists the knockout
(extending the T5-immunity shape).

## MINELAYER — VANISH

A charge buys TRULY UNDETECTABLE stealth for its duration —
the purchased exception to STEALTH.md's proximity grading;
escalation in duration / charges / regen, DRAFT.

**Firing rule (Adam, final form):** inside the zone there is
NO visible tell at all — *"a literal muzzle flash would not
be visible inside that zone."* A projectile is perceived
only as it EXITS the zone; what firing betrays is the zone's
EDGE/AREA — a neighborhood to investigate (BOTS.md's Search
is its honest object) — never the hull's position. The hull
stays at zero detection for the duration, firing or not.
BackGround's phrasing for the read: "what firing spends is
not the ship's invisibility but the neighborhood's
innocence." Adam's judgment for the comment: firing from
perfect stealth is "stupid on the part of the stealth craft,
unless it's confident."

VANISH is an AREA-level instrument, distinct from ordinary
cloak — STEALTH.md's periscope reveal (the hull revealed on
fire) remains the ordinary cloak's own law; any shared
plumbing may render the AREA marker only, never a position
reveal.

## HAULER — GRAVITY LASSO

Adam accepted and extended. Drags a scrap cluster or wreck
field to her; can also lasso an ENEMY SHIP — a tractor hold
so allies can gang up.

**Size is the restraint** (Adam: "size is an issue for the
lasso let's build on that restraint"). The contest reads the
real size field — hull size = 15 + 2/step, from 15 (fresh
Skiff) to 41 (maxed Dreadnought) — and the hauler's own
growth puts her at the top of the field at every tier: "her
size IS her authority." Ratio bands DRAFT (hauler effective
size ÷ target size):

- **≥ 1.6 REEL** — hauled bodily; thrust at steep discount.
- **1.25–1.6 DRAG** — real pull; thrust resists.
- **0.8–1.25 HOLD/SLOW** — parity: no translation against a
  thrusting victim; the tether taxes speed and turn.
- **< 0.8 HOLD-ONLY** — she can only anchor HERSELF to the
  target.

Her WINCH grades her effective size ×1.0 / 1.1 / 1.2 / 1.3 /
1.45 (T1–T5, DRAFT) — yet a maxed Dreadnought still
out-masses a T5 Atlas into hold-only. Against scrap and
wreck clusters there is no thrust and no contest: free reel
at any size — the day job. The victim keeps its guns in
every band. The tether breaks on range, on sustained heavy
fire, and on VANISH.

## CARRIER — RESERVE LAUNCH

A charge instantly launches a TEMPORARY Skiff escort that
fights beside the wing and burns out on a timer; escalation
in charges and duration (T5: launched in pairs). The wing
itself is SHIP_PROGRESSION.md's subject; the Launch is the
signature, not a purchase.

## CRUISER — MISSILE SALVO

Adam's amendment, Battlestar Galactica register — replaces
a discarded main-gun concept that duplicated the Rail Lance.
A banked charge fires a broadside of homing missiles: an
EVENT, not a mount. Seeker Torpedoes remain a loadout
weapon; the Salvo is the line's crown. (The fiction was
already aboard: the T4 cruiser is named BATTLESTAR.)
